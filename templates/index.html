KTM Event Planner

KTM Event Planner is located on the third floor of a restored trade hall in the old quarter, built as a meeting point for the region's shared and separate histories. The center runs a rotating program of exhibitions, workshops and performances developed with cultural institutions across Southeast Asia, open to residents and visitors alike.
